Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Fabulous App Developer provides the Peptides2GO iPhone app. This policy explains how information is handled when you keep a personal log of peptides and testosterone.

Peptides2GO does not recommend a dose, a schedule, or how to take a shot.

Information you enter

The names, amounts, schedules, and history you enter stay on this device. The app does not create an account and does not upload that log to a server. The app does not sell your information. Copies in an iPhone backup are controlled by your Apple settings.

This version

This version does not show ads and does not offer a purchase. It does not request an advertisement and does not send your log to an advertising or payment service.

The app includes Google advertising software so a later version can show a banner. That software is not asked to load an ad in this version. If a later version shows a banner, it would be a non-personalized ad. Your log would still stay on the device and would not be sent to Google for advertising. Read the Google Privacy Policy.

A later purchase

A later version may offer an optional monthly subscription through Apple that removes the banner. Any charge would be billed by Apple. Peptides2GO would not receive your card number. Read the Apple Privacy Policy.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message you choose to send. We use that information to reply. Please do not include passwords, payment-card numbers, or medical details.

Children

Peptides2GO is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date above is the latest revision.
